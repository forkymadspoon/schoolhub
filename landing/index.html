What makes SchoolHub different

Built for Singapore parents
who want more than a planner

Most study apps give you a timer. SchoolHub gives you a complete picture — and a plan that actually adapts.

AI-POWERED

Adaptive scheduling that actually learns

SchoolHub parses MOE syllabi and generates a week-by-week plan around your child's school calendar, exam dates, and weekly study hours. Automatically regenerates when things change.

SEN-AWARE

Designed for every learner

ADHD bites are capped at 5 minutes. Autism Spectrum profiles get 48-hour schedule-change notice. Every adaptation is evidence-based — grounded in ABA, CBT, and Vygotsky ZPD frameworks.

WELLBEING

Mental health guardrails built in

A rule-based engine watches for overload, burnout, and exam anxiety — and surfaces gentle suggestions to you as the parent. Always overridable. Never diagnostic. Never alarmist.
